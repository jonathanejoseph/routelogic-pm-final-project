# Final Project Presentation

> ★ Final submission — the deck you submit to the LMS to qualify for certification.

Your final project is a single deck (Google Slides or PDF) that tells the story of your product concept end-to-end, built from the six deliverable files in this repo.

## How to build it

1. Open the **Final Project Deliverables Template** (linked in your LMS / Project Brief) and click **Use Template** to get your own copy.
2. Fill each slide from the matching deliverable file in this repo:
   - **Slide 1 — Problem Hook, Value Prop & Hypothesis** ← `01-product-thinking/problem-hook.md` (+ hypothesis from `03-analytics/`)
   - **Slide 2 — Competitive Analysis & Journey Map** ← `02-discovery/competitive-and-journey.md`
   - **Slide 3 — Prioritization, Roadmap, PRD & Prototype** ← `04-roadmap/roadmap-prd-prototype.md`
   - **Slide 4 — Experimentation Plan** ← `05-experimentation/experimentation-plan.md`
   - **Slide 5 — GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard** ← `06-launch/gtm-and-dashboard.md`
   - **Slide 6 — Individual Insights** ← `06-launch/individual-insights.md`
3. _(Optional)_ Make it your own with an AI presentation tool — paste your six files into **Gamma**, **Canva**, **Claude**, or **ChatGPT** and prompt a polished deck.
4. Review, then submit your own copy to the LMS within **7 days** of your cohort ending.

## The final showcase _(optional)_

During the last ~30 minutes of Module 6, three to five volunteers present (5 min each):

1. **Demo the core logic** — show your wireframes and how your concept addresses the user's needs.
2. **Defend your artifacts** — walk through one or two you're most proud of and the trade-offs behind them.
3. **Share your insights** — close with your friction points, key learnings, and aha moments.
